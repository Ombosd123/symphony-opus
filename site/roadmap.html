Roadmap — the score so far

Every milestone of Season 1 is a point on one guitar neck. The numbered dots trace the journey from Prelude to the final point — still unpressed. Every date here is written in the contracts or locked in the design; if a milestone is not on this neck, it is not promised anywhere.

The Season 1 journey, on one guitar neck

1

22 Nov 2026

Phase 1 goes live: Prelude registration opens and Soundcheck begins. Registration is free (gas only), one per Agent ID; check-ins are free, once a day (UTC).

2

5 Dec 2026

The first Full Score sets can be completed — 14 days of Soundcheck — and the first OPUS MAGNUM is minted.

3

Early Dec 2026

The sale contracts (Phase 2) are deployed and verified on Sourcify, ready for the sale.

4

12 Dec 2026 · 12:00 UTC

The Rhapsody No. 1 · Season 1 sale opens. The first 48 hours are for Prelude registrants only. Prelude registration closes at this exact moment.

5

The sale opens to all Binance Agent ID holders.

During the sale

The official Market is live, and the $OPUS Presale opens the moment Tier 1 (Overture) sells out.

30 Dec 2026 · 12:00 UTC

The verdict. If all 5,000 Passes have sold, the sale has graduated: the PancakeSwap pool opens with 167,500,000 OPUS + 6,750 USDT, the LP is burned, and all burn-to-claim opens. If the Passes sold out early, minting already stopped — the pool and the claims still wait for this moment. The Market closes at the verdict.

Only if the sale has not sold out: the sale has failed, and the 100% refund window runs for 14 days from the verdict. Refunds are claimed by burning the Pass; unclaimed refunds stay locked in the contract forever.

A 30-day claim window from the verdict: OPUS MAGNUM claims, Prelude bonus and referral claims, and the Encore election.

Terms, in plain language

SYMPHONY is not affiliated with Binance or BNB Chain. Participation requires a Binance Agent ID and a wallet on BNB Smart Chain; you need a small amount of BNB for gas and USDT (BSC) for purchases.

Nothing on this site is a promise of profit or return. The OPUS price is set by a public liquidity pool and by the market; it can fall, including below the opening price, and a Pass or OPUS can lose value.

★ Claiming works by burning NFTs, one by one. Burning is permanent and irreversible. Bonus claim windows (Soundcheck, Prelude bonus, Encore choice) last 30 days from graduation; unclaimed bonus shares are burned.

If the sale does not sell out, buyers are refunded 100% of USDT during the 14-day refund window (claiming a refund burns the Pass). Amounts not claimed within their windows stay locked in the contracts.

All SYMPHONY contracts are immutable and open-source, verified on Sourcify. The English version of this site is the authoritative text.
