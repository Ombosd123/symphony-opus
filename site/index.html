Soundcheck — show up every day

Check in free, once a day (UTC), with any Agent ID. Keep the streak alive and badges are minted to you automatically. Complete the full 14-day set and you also receive the OPUS MAGNUM — your claim instrument for a pro-rata share of the 30,000,000 OPUS Soundcheck pool (capped at 60,000 OPUS per person), payable if the sale graduates.

Missed exactly one day? A repair costs 0.1 USDT: 0.05 USDT goes to the treasury (this share is not returned, even if the sale fails) and 0.05 USDT is held as a pool share, recorded under your address — it joins the pool if the sale graduates, or you can claim it back during the refund window if the sale fails. A gap of more than one day cannot be repaired; the streak restarts.

The sale — Rhapsody No. 1 · Season 1

5,000 Mint Passes, max 10 per wallet, paid in USDT. The sale opens Saturday 12 Dec 2026, 12:00 UTC — the first 24 hours are for Prelude registrants only, then it opens to every Agent ID holder. Minting runs 18 days, until the verdict on Wednesday 30 Dec 2026, 12:00 UTC. If all 5,000 Passes have sold by then, the sale has graduated: the pool is seeded (167,500,000 OPUS + 6,750 USDT on PancakeSwap V2, LP burned) and all burn-to-claim opens. If the Passes sell out early, minting simply stops — the pool and claims still wait for the verdict. If even one Pass remains unsold at the verdict, the sale fails and all buyers are refunded 100%.

Claiming OPUS means burning the NFT, one at a time: burn a Pass and choose Premiere (100% at once) or Encore (50% at once, 50% vesting over 60 days, plus an Encore bonus and — on your first Encore claim — a soulbound SCHERZO key NFT: exactly one per wallet, never transferable, the key to a guaranteed Overture seat in Scherzo (the second movement · Season 2, planned) if it opens). ★ Burning is permanent — a burned NFT is destroyed and cannot be recovered.

Market Coming Soon

The official SYMPHONY market is the only way a Pass can change hands. Passes are semi-soulbound — free transfers are not possible, and a Pass cannot be listed on third-party marketplaces. The market opens together with the sale, on 12 Dec 2026, 12:00 UTC, and stays open until the verdict on 30 Dec 2026, 12:00 UTC, when it closes — whether the sale has graduated or failed.

  • Only Passes that have not been claimed can be traded. Payment is in USDT.
  • Buyers must hold a Binance Agent ID.
  • Sellers set their own price, with a minimum floor of 80% of what that Pass cost: Tier 1 minimum 0.8 USDT, Tier 2 minimum 1.2 USDT, Tier 3 minimum 1.6 USDT.
  • A 5% fee is taken from the seller's proceeds only when a sale completes, and it goes to the TreasuryVault.
  • OPUS MAGNUM can also be traded here during the sale period, at a freely set price.
  • If the sale fails, the market freezes automatically: listings return to their sellers, and refunds follow the current holder of each Pass.
  • When it opens, the market page will show each Pass's reference claim value (claim amount × pool price) as information only — it is not a price guide.

Terms, in plain language

SYMPHONY is not affiliated with Binance or BNB Chain. Participation requires a Binance Agent ID and a wallet on BNB Smart Chain; you need a small amount of BNB for gas and USDT (BSC) for purchases.

Nothing on this site is a promise of profit or return. The OPUS price is set by a public liquidity pool and by the market; it can fall, including below the opening price, and a Pass or OPUS can lose value.

★ Claiming works by burning NFTs, one by one. Burning is permanent and irreversible. Bonus claim windows (Soundcheck, Prelude bonus, Encore choice) last 30 days from graduation; unclaimed bonus shares are burned.

If the sale does not sell out, buyers are refunded 100% of USDT during the 14-day refund window (claiming a refund burns the Pass). Amounts not claimed within their windows stay locked in the contracts.

All SYMPHONY contracts are immutable and open-source, verified on Sourcify. The English version of this site is the authoritative text.
