Frequently asked questions

Every answer below is a fact taken from the SYMPHONY contracts and design specification. If an answer is not here, it is not promised anywhere.

What is SYMPHONY?

A community fair launch of the OPUS token ($OPUS) for Binance Agent ID holders on BNB Chain. Its principle is Open Score: the treasury split, escrow and refund rules are executed by immutable contracts that anyone can read on-chain. No one plays a symphony alone — the project is built together by its participants.

Who can take part?

Anyone holding a Binance Agent ID. Registration, check-ins, primary purchases and market purchases check your Agent ID in the official registry. Claiming and refunds do not require an active Agent ID — holding the NFT is enough.

What do I need in my wallet?

A wallet on BNB Smart Chain (chainId 56), a small amount of BNB for gas, and USDT on BSC (contract 0x55d398326f99059fF775485246999027B3197955, 18 decimals) for anything paid. Prelude registration and Soundcheck check-ins are free — gas only.

What is Prelude?

Free registration, open now until 12 Dec 2026, 12:00 UTC. One per Agent ID, no obligation to buy. You receive a soulbound Prelude Pass. The sale opens only if at least 500 registrations are in by that moment; below 500, the sale does not open. Registrants enter the sale 24 hours early, and those who buy at least one Pass share the 15,000,000 OPUS Prelude bonus pool equally per person if the sale graduates (claim within 30 days of graduation, by burning the Prelude Pass).

What is Soundcheck?

A free daily check-in, open to every Agent ID holder. Streak badges are minted at 1, 3, 7 and 14 consecutive days (UTC). At day 14 you also receive the OPUS MAGNUM, which claims a pro-rata share of the 30,000,000 OPUS Soundcheck pool (max 60,000 OPUS per person) if the sale graduates. Claims burn the Magnum and must be made within 30 days of graduation. The season runs until the verdict, on 30 Dec 2026, 12:00 UTC.

I missed a day. What happens to my streak?

If exactly one day was missed, you may either restart for free, or repair the streak for 0.1 USDT: 0.05 USDT goes to the treasury and is not returned even if the sale fails; 0.05 USDT is recorded as your pool share — it joins the pool if the sale graduates, or you can claim it back during the refund window if the sale fails. A gap of two or more days cannot be repaired.

How does the sale work?

5,000 Mint Passes, max 10 per wallet, priced by sales order: Tier 1 Overture 1 USDT → 78,000 OPUS; Tier 2 Crescendo 1.5 USDT → 65,000 OPUS; Tier 3 Finale 2 USDT → 57,000 OPUS. It opens 12 Dec 2026, 12:00 UTC (first 24 hours: Prelude registrants only) and minting runs 18 days, until the verdict on 30 Dec 2026, 12:00 UTC. A full sell-out raises 7,500 USDT: 90% seeds the pool, 10% goes to the TreasuryVault.

What is the opening price, and is it a promise?

At graduation the pool is seeded with 167,500,000 OPUS and 6,750 USDT, which prints an opening price of $0.0000403 per OPUS. At that print, a Tier 1 claim is worth $3.14, Tier 2 $2.62 and Tier 3 $2.30. These are arithmetic values of the opening print only — not promises. The market price moves from the first trade and can fall below the opening price; if many holders sell at once, the pool price falls quickly.

What if the sale does not sell out?

Then it fails — even at 4,999 of 5,000. The outcome is decided at the verdict on 30 Dec 2026, 12:00 UTC. There is no pool, and every buyer can claim a 100% USDT refund during the 14-day window (30 Dec 2026 – 13 Jan 2027) by burning their Pass. Unclaimed refunds stay locked in the contract. Presale buyers are also refunded 100%.

What is the presale?

A direct sale of 5,000,000 OPUS (taken from the team allocation) at $0.0001 per OPUS. It is openly the most expensive route in — what it buys is allocation certainty and OPUS directly, without an NFT or a burn. It opens only once Tier 1 is sold out, is capped at 50 USDT per wallet, has no Prelude priority, and splits 90/10 like the main sale. If Tier 1 never sells out, the presale never opens.

How do claims work? Why must I burn my NFT?

Every OPUS claim burns its NFT, strictly one by one — there is no bulk burn. Burn a Pass and choose in the same transaction: Premiere (100% immediately) or Encore (50% immediately + 50% vesting linearly over 60 days + a share of the 5,000,000 OPUS Encore pool, capped at +50% of the vested part, + a soulbound SCHERZO key NFT on your first Encore claim — exactly one per wallet, never transferable — the key to a guaranteed Overture seat in Scherzo, the planned Season 2 movement, if it opens). ★ Burning is permanent: the NFT is destroyed. Pass Premiere claims have no deadline; the Encore choice, Magnum claims and Prelude bonus claims close 30 days after graduation, and unclaimed bonus shares are burned.

Can I trade my Pass or Magnum?

Only through the official market on this site — free transfers are blocked by the contracts. The market charges a 5% fee taken from the seller's proceeds, paid in USDT, and buyers must hold an Agent ID. A Pass can only be listed before it is claimed, and its price cannot be set below 80% of what was paid for it. The OPUS MAGNUM can also be traded on the official market during the sale period. Milestone badges can never be traded.

Where does the team's money go?

Every treasury commission — the 10% sale split, the 5% market fee, the 0.05 USDT team share of streak repairs, the 10% presale split — first accumulates in the TreasuryVault contract. Only the project treasury wallet can claim from the vault, and that rule is immutable in the contract. The team's 15,000,000 OPUS vesting (30-day cliff, then linear over 365 days) also pays to the treasury wallet.

Can the OPUS price go down? Can I lose money?

Yes. OPUS trades in a public pool; its price can fall below the opening print and a Pass or OPUS can lose part or all of its value. Nothing in SYMPHONY promises profit, yield or a buy-back. Only take part with funds you can afford to lose.

Is SYMPHONY affiliated with Binance?

No. SYMPHONY is an independent community project that uses the public Binance Agent ID registry as its entry gate. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or part of Binance or BNB Chain.

What is Your Melody?

Your Melody is a unique 8-note phrase that belongs to your wallet address. It is derived publicly from the address itself: take the keccak-256 hash of the address's 20 bytes; the first eight bytes, in order, choose the notes from the C major pentatonic scale (C D E G A across two octaves), and the next eight bytes set each note's length (half a beat or a full beat). Anyone can recompute the melody of any address with this same recipe — nothing is stored, assigned in secret or kept on a server, in line with Open Score. Your Melody never plays on its own: it only sounds when you press Play, and you can stop it at any moment. It is an identity and experience feature only: it carries no claim, no bonus, no allocation and no monetary value.

Is there a referral program?

There are two separate referrals. (1) Prelude referral (phase 1): after you register, you get a personal link. For every person who registers for Prelude through your link, 100 OPUS is credited to you from a 5,000,000 OPUS referral pool carved out of the Prelude bonus allocation (the flat Prelude bonus pool is the remaining 15,000,000 OPUS). At most 50 registrations per referrer are counted, so the most one referrer can be credited is 5,000 OPUS. The referrer is recorded on-chain when the person registers — the on-chain record alone is what counts; the person you brought does not need to buy anything for the credit to exist. The credit is paid only if the sale graduates, through the same Merkle distributor and 30-day claim window as the other community rewards, and unclaimed shares are burned. Registering through a link costs the new registrant nothing. (2) Sale referral (phase 2): during the sale, 5,000 OPUS for every Pass sold through your referral, paid from the separate 25,000,000 OPUS referral allocation; the referrer must hold at least one Pass. Referral accrual stops at a pool cap of 15,000,000 OPUS; the rest of the allocation funds the AgentQuest badge perk (see the next question).

What are the seasons — Rhapsody and Scherzo?

SYMPHONY is composed as a series of movements, and each movement is one season. Rhapsody No. 1 — the first movement · Season 1 — is the current edition: the Prelude registration, Soundcheck and the Pass sale described on this site. Scherzo — the second movement · Season 2 — is planned as the next edition, but planned is not promised: no dates, numbers or mechanics for Scherzo exist, and none are announced. The one fact about Scherzo that is already fixed is written in the Season 1 contracts: every wallet that claims through the Encore path receives a soulbound SCHERZO key NFT — exactly one per wallet, minted on its first Encore claim — which is the key to a guaranteed Overture (Tier 1) seat in Scherzo if it opens; the key registry is recorded on-chain.

Is there a perk for AgentQuest badge holders?

Yes — an official partnership perk, written into the sale contract itself. If the wallet that claims a Pass holds at least one AgentQuest badge at the moment of the claim, it receives a bonus of 5,000 OPUS for that Pass, paid in the same transaction as the claim — on the Premiere path and on the Encore path alike. At most 10 Passes per wallet earn the bonus. Bonuses are paid from a fenced pool of 10,000,000 OPUS inside the 25,000,000 OPUS referral allocation, in claim order while the pool lasts. The sale contract checks the badge balance directly on the AgentQuest badge contract (0x26AD277193ff31BD086a47BF7B1d25a93573c989) — there is no list and no manual review. Like every claim, the bonus is paid only if the sale graduates. The pool divides exactly into 2,000 whole bonuses, so a remainder below 5,000 OPUS can never occur; once the pool is empty, claims simply proceed without a bonus, and the bonus logic can never block a claim.

Terms, in plain language

SYMPHONY is not affiliated with Binance or BNB Chain. Participation requires a Binance Agent ID and a wallet on BNB Smart Chain; you need a small amount of BNB for gas and USDT (BSC) for purchases.

Nothing on this site is a promise of profit or return. The OPUS price is set by a public liquidity pool and by the market; it can fall, including below the opening price, and a Pass or OPUS can lose value.

★ Claiming works by burning NFTs, one by one. Burning is permanent and irreversible. Bonus claim windows (Soundcheck, Prelude bonus, Encore choice) last 30 days from graduation; unclaimed bonus shares are burned.

If the sale does not sell out, buyers are refunded 100% of USDT during the 14-day refund window (claiming a refund burns the Pass). Amounts not claimed within their windows stay locked in the contracts.

All SYMPHONY contracts are immutable and open-source, verified on Sourcify. The English version of this site is the authoritative text.
